🔬 Methodology & Intentional Bias Disclosure

This analysis is designed to test a hypothesis: even under the most AI-unfavorable assumptions possible, dietary water savings still dominate. To ensure intellectual honesty, we explicitly disclose our estimation strategy:

AI INFLATED ⬆️ For AI/data center water, we use the highest published estimates at every level — 500 ml per prompt [10] (vs Google's disclosed 0.26 ml [12] or OpenAI's claimed 0.3 ml [13]), maximum projected growth rates [15][16], and full scope 1+2+3 accounting (on-site cooling, electricity generation water, and chip manufacturing).

VEGAN CONSERVATIVE ⬇️ For vegan diet savings, we use conservative estimates. Some studies report up to 55% water reduction [5]; others show as low as 7% for a Mediterranean comparison [9]. We use ~45% reduction as our baseline — the lower end of most U.S.-focused studies [6][7][8]. The Loma Linda University study found vegetarians save 54% [5]; we round down further for vegans to account for water-intensive plant crops.

The result: AI numbers are inflated by roughly 10–100× above median estimates, while vegan savings are held at the conservative floor. Despite this, the dietary impact still dominates by 1–2 orders of magnitude.
